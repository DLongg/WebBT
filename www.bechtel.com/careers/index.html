Additional Career Programs

As global investment in construction surges, we need passionate individuals ready to help us build the future. To meet this moment, Bechtel offers a range of dynamic career pathways for people with different life experiences, including our Veterans Program, Internship and Apprenticeship Programs, New Graduates Program, and Returners Program. These pathways help us recruit, support, and develop the best talent no matter where they are in their career journey.

New Graduates Program

For recent or upcoming graduates, we offer development opportunities that will help you launch your career in the engineering and construction industry. You’ll have many resources available to you to grow your skills, learn from industry experts, and contribute to building a better world.

Internship Program

Our Internship Program allows students to go beyond the classroom and gain first-hand knowledge of the engineering, procurement, and construction industry, while also making a positive impact for our customers and communities. These roles, which are available for current students who will graduate in the next 1-2 years, involve technical training, mentorship, educational resources, and networking events, culminating with an intern showcase.

Veterans Program

With their leadership skills, technical training, rigor, and mission-focused approach, military veterans are ideal candidates for many positions in our industry. We provide skill development, training, apprenticeship, and quality career opportunities in the construction industry to veterans and service members through our Military to Project Program and NABTU’s Helmets to Hardhats Program.

Apprenticeship Program

In addition to offering our own Apprenticeship Programs using the National Center for Construction Education and Research (NCCER) curriculum, we work with NABTU and TradeFutures to advance Apprenticeship Readiness Programs (ARPs). ARPs helps women, veterans, and people of color launch careers in the construction industry.

Returners Program

Our Re-Entry in STEM Employment Program (RISE) provides a supportive pathway for colleagues to smoothly transition back into the workforce. This comprehensive program helps colleagues regain momentum, build new skills, and unlock opportunities for professional growth.

Life at Bechtel

We work every day to foster a culture that values and seeks out many perspectives, and encourages colleagues to explore, think innovatively, and grow professionally. We’re proud to offer numerous benefits and opportunities to our colleagues to invest in their success and empower them to reach their full potential.

Benefits & Perks

The health and well-being of our colleagues and their families is our top priority. The benefits we offer vary from country to country, based on local practices, customs, and statutory conditions, but may include competitive compensation, medical benefits, retirement plans, life insurance, and paid time off. As a Bechtel colleague, you also have access to a number of professional development opportunities, including virtual courses through Bechtel University.

Diversity & Inclusion

We work to ensure that every colleague feels that they belong as part of “One Team,” and are respected and rewarded for what they bring, supported in pursuing their goals, invested in our values and purpose, and given the opportunity to thrive.

Industry Groups & Associations

We support many partners advancing STEM fields, including the Society of Women Engineers, the National Society of Black Engineers, the Society of Hispanic Engineers, and the National Action Council for Minorities in Engineering — among others. In fact, for many, Bechtel was a founding member, supporting most of these efforts for more than five decades.

Business Resource Groups

Bechtel’s employee-led Business Resource Groups (BRGs) bring together various communities of individuals with shared backgrounds, experience, and interests. These voluntary organizations enable colleagues to support each other and create opportunities to connect, collaborate, and celebrate with one another. We have thriving BRGs today representing our Black, Asian, Latino, LGBTQ+, disabled, and military veterans communities, as well as a group for colleagues focused on professional development.

Volunteership & Community Involvement

Our Bechtel colleagues around the world are active members in their communities. They are deeply motivated to give back by working with local organizations to improve lives and leave a lasting positive legacy. Bechtel colleagues are also able to join a bechtel.org project, our impact-driven infrastructure arm, then reintegrate back into Bechtel when assignments are complete, allowing them to transfer their knowledge and experience into commercial projects.

Signature Programs

We partner with global non-government organizations that promote education or use engineering and construction skills to improve the quality of life: DiscoverE, which aims to grow the engineering profession through volunteering, education, and student outreach; Engineers Without Borders, which supports community-driven sustainable design and development programs; FIRST® Robotics, which provides mentor-based programs that build STEM skills.
